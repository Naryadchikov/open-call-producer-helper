# Onboarding

Open-Call Producer can first run a fictional demo so you can see the approval and validation workflow without sharing personal work.

For a real call, provide the organiser's original page or complete rules. The bot will research requirements first, explain what is verified or unresolved, and ask only for the minimum facts needed.

When artwork selection is relevant, you can upload finished preview copies. The bot may suggest call-relevant groupings, but you approve the final selection and order.

Before any submission copies are created, the bot shows the exact proposed filenames, processing, packaged text, fee calculation and manual checks and waits for your explicit approval.

It never submits, pays, publishes, accepts terms or alters originals.
